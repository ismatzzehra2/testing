Our Story

About Rasioe

Nestled in the heart of Alamdar Chowk, Skardu, Rasioe is more than a restaurant — it is a destination. Born from a vision to bring world-class fine dining to the mountains of Gilgit-Baltistan, we blend local heritage with global technique.

Every ingredient is hand-selected. Every plate is composed with intent. From the wild trout of the Indus to the apricots of Hunza, our menu is a love letter to the land we call home.

Directions for Visitors

Travelling to Skardu? Here's how to find us at Alamdar Chowk.

01
By Air

Fly to Skardu Airport (KDU) from Islamabad — daily PIA and AirSial flights. The airport is 15 minutes from Alamdar Chowk.

02
By Road

Take the Karakoram Highway (KKH) from Islamabad via Gilgit. The journey is 20-24 hours. Alternatively, book a NATCO van directly to Skardu.

03
Local Transport

From anywhere in Skardu city, take a rickshaw or taxi to Alamdar Chowk. Rasioe is located in the main commercial area — you can't miss our exterior signage.

04
Parking

Free parking available for guests directly in front of the restaurant. Valet service available for private events.
