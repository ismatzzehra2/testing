Our Story

About Rasioe

Nestled in the heart of Alamdar Chowk, Skardu, Rasioe is more than a restaurant — it is a destination. Born from a vision to bring world-class fine dining to the mountains of Gilgit-Baltistan, we blend local heritage with global technique.

Every ingredient is hand-selected. Every plate is composed with intent. From the wild trout of the Indus to the apricots of Hunza, our menu is a love letter to the land we call home.

Zaffar Abbas

Founder & Owner — "Cooking is my soul's expression. Every guest is family."
